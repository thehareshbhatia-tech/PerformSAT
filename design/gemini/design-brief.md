# PerformSAT - Design Brief

## Product Purpose
PerformSAT is an AI-powered, digital SAT preparation platform. Its core mission is to help students improve their SAT scores through personalized practice tests, targeted skill diagnostics, and real-time AI tutoring, mimicking the digital SAT (Bluebook) experience while adding deep educational value.

## Target Audience
- High school students preparing for the digital SAT.
- They are anxious about the test, looking for quick score improvements, and often easily distracted.
- Need a clear, encouraging, and intuitive interface that doesn't feel like "more homework" but rather an engaging, gamified challenge.

## Core Jobs-to-be-Done
- Take full-length, timed digital SAT practice tests (reading/writing and math).
- Review detailed diagnostic reports to understand weak areas.
- Drill specific skills (e.g., algebra, grammar) via short lesson browsers and practice lessons.
- Get instant help from an AI tutor when stuck on a specific question.
- Track progress over time towards a target college score goal.

## Accessibility Baseline
- WCAG AA contrast for text elements.
- Full keyboard navigation support (critical for students using laptops).
- Clear focus states, no reliance on color alone for conveying meaning (e.g., correct/incorrect indicators must have icons, not just green/red).

## Platform Constraints
- Web responsive first: The primary environment is Desktop/Laptop/Tablet (as students typically take SATs and study on larger screens).
- Mobile adaptations are required for quick study sessions, dashboard viewing, and notifications, but not the primary testing environment.

# R&W Authenticity Calibration — Baseline Ratings

**Status:** Empty rating sheet — to be filled in by raters.

- **SC** — Stem clarity
- **PA** — Passage authenticity
- **DQ** — Distractor quality
- **DC** — Difficulty calibration (vs. claimed band)
- **CB** — Overall CB-grade (would this fit unobtrusively in PT 4-11?)

See `methodology.md` for the rating playbook and dimension rubrics.
A short note explains any rating ≤3 or anything notable about the item. Aggregate analysis is at the bottom (compute after all 40 items are rated).

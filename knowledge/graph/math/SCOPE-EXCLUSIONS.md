# Math graph — deliberate scope exclusions

Not a concept node. Records topics deliberately left OUT of the graph, with reasons,
so future audits don't re-flag them as gaps.

- **Complex-number arithmetic** (the imaginary unit i, powers of i, conjugates,
  complex division) — dropped from the digital SAT's tested domains; excluded from
  the graph. If bank items surface that genuinely require it, add a compact node
  then — do not pre-build.
- **Math notation primer** (≈, ∞, ±, set braces, segment/parallel/perpendicular/
  angle/arc notation) — presumed student-known; where a symbol matters to a concept,
  it is taught in context inside that concept's node rather than as a standalone
  reference node.
- **Imaginary-unit content in legacy bank items** — any such items route to bank
  review/retirement, not to the graph. The graph does not grow nodes to cover
  content the exam no longer tests.

SAT Math, solved in 8 seconds.

Two factors means two zeros: 14 and −19. The minimum is always halfway between them, so average the zeros and you are done. Most students expand the whole thing first and lose a minute.

Watch for the sign: (−19 − 14)/2 gives −33/2, and that wrong answer is sitting right there in the choices.

# The SEVA Creator Playbook: How to Actually Go Viral

Read this before you post anything. It takes 10 minutes and it's the
difference between videos that hit 100,000 views and videos stuck at 200
forever. None of this is theory — it's the system behind creator accounts
doing millions of views, adapted for SAT content.

The most important thing to understand: **going viral is not luck, and it's
not talent. It's a process.** Creators who follow it hit. Creators who skip
steps stall — and then blame the content, when the content was never the
problem.

## Step 1: Warm up your account before your first post (3–4 days)

This is the step almost everyone skips, and it's the #1 reason good content
dies at 200 views. The algorithm decides who sees your videos before you
ever post — based on how your account behaves. The warm-up tells it "my
audience is students preparing for the SAT."

For 3–4 days before your first post:

1. **Scroll like your future follower.** Search and watch content around:
   SAT prep, digital SAT, study with me, studytok, SAT tips, study
   motivation, PSAT, college admissions. Let your For You page fill up with
   it. This teaches the algorithm who you are.
2. **Like and save sparingly — about 1 in 20 videos.** Liking everything
   looks like a bot. Watch like a real person: rewatch the good ones, skip
   the boring ones.
3. **Follow 1–2 study creators a day** (accounts with 5K–150K followers),
   naturally, as they show up in your feed. Never follow a big batch at
   once.
4. **Repost 1–2 study posts a day** — mix big viral ones with mid-size ones
   (10K–100K views). This tells the algorithm which content world you
   belong to.

Then post. If the warm-up was done right, your first 5 posts should each
clear 1,000 views. If they don't, the fix is redoing the warm-up — not
changing your content.

**One hard rule: do not follow, like, or comment on other SEVA creators'
accounts.** It sounds friendly, but it makes the algorithm show all of us to
the same small audience — and it can flag every account involved. Stay in
your own lane; that's what makes the network work.

## Step 2: Copy what's already winning (don't invent)

Virality is engineered from proven patterns. Before you film anything, find
3–5 study videos in your niche with huge view counts and study them like
homework:

- What's the hook in the first 2 seconds?
- How long is the video? What sound is it using?
- What does the text overlay say, and where is it on the screen?
- When does the app (or product) show up — and for how long?
- What happens in the last 3 seconds that makes people share it?

Then replicate **exactly** — same pacing, same length, same music style,
same text placement — with your face, your voice, and SEVA as the subject.
Not a loose version. An exact execution. Once you find a format of yours
that consistently clears 1,000–5,000 views, ride it. Repetition is not lazy;
repetition is the strategy.

## Step 3: Make SEVA the punchline, not the ad

Only about 1 in 3 of your posts should mention SEVA at all. The rest build
your audience. And when SEVA does appear, it should live INSIDE the
content, not on top of it.

The test: **if you could cut SEVA out of the video and the video still
works, it's a promo, not a post.** The best integration is when the app IS
the payoff — "I kept missing these questions and couldn't figure out why…"
and the reveal is the SEVA screen showing exactly why. The viewer's next
thought should be "wait, what app is that?" — that's the moment your link
earns money.

Your best raw material is the app itself: a 15-second screen recording of
the "why you missed it" diagnosis, a score screen, a brutal trap question —
plus your reaction or a voiceover — is a complete video. Before/after works
especially hard in our niche: the score you started with vs where you are
now, the question you got wrong vs finally understanding the trap. Make the
contrast big and obvious.

## Step 4: The small details that decide everything

These sound trivial. They're not — each one measurably moves views:

- **Hooks: specific beats generic, every time.** "i kept getting 1200s
  until i saw WHY i was missing questions" beats "SAT tips" forever. Put
  hook text small and off to the side, not centered and big.
- **Trending audio is not background noise — it's distribution.** The
  algorithm pushes videos using trending sounds to a much bigger first
  audience. Check what's trending before every post.
- **Post between 6 and 10pm** in US time — that's when students are on
  their phones. Morning and afternoon posts consistently underperform.
- **Test your captions.** Two captions that mean the same thing can perform
  completely differently. When in doubt, make it personal and first-person.
- **The first 2 seconds are 80% of the result.** If the hook doesn't stop
  the scroll, nothing else in the video matters.

## Step 5: Keep going — especially after you hit

Consistency is what the algorithm rewards most. Post at least 3–5 times a
week, every week. And here's the counterintuitive one: **when a video blows
up, do not stop to celebrate.** Accounts that go quiet after a hit get reset
to baseline within about 72 hours. The days right after a viral video are
the most valuable posting days you'll ever have — the algorithm is actively
looking for more from you. Feed it.

## If you're stuck at 200 views

It's almost never the content. Run this checklist in order:

1. Was the account properly warmed up for 3–4 days before the first post?
2. Are you posting in the 6–10pm window with trending audio?
3. Is every video using a different hook (not the same opener recycled)?
4. Have you interacted with other SEVA creators' accounts? (If yes — stop.)
5. Are you copying a proven format exactly, or a loose version of it?

If all five check out and it still won't move, message us — we'll look at it
with you. That's what the direct line is for.

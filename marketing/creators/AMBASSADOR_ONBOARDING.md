# SEVA Creator Partner Program

**Get paid real money to post study content. No follower minimum. No fees. No scripts.**

You post study content in your own voice, on your own account. We pay you cash
for every subscriber it brings in — plus a bonus just for starting. We're
selecting a small founding group of creator partners for this fall SAT season.

## Why this beats everything else out there

- **Big test-prep companies pay their affiliates 5–10%** — Princeton Review
  works out to about $25 a sale. **SEVA pays $70 cash per annual signup.**
- **TikTok's Creator Rewards program requires 10,000 followers and 100,000
  monthly views** before you earn a cent — and then pays roughly $40–100 a
  month. **One SEVA signup pays $70. From day one, at any follower count.**
- **Most student ambassador programs pay in merch, gift cards, or
  "exposure."** SEVA pays actual money, every month, straight to your Zelle,
  PayPal, or Venmo. No minimum payout.

## What is SEVA?

SEVA (sevaprep.com) is an SAT prep app built around one idea: **we don't just
tell students what the right answer was — we tell them why they missed it.**
Every practice test looks and feels like the real digital SAT. When a student
misses a question, SEVA figures out why — a skill gap, a trap answer, or a
careless slip — then builds their study plan around their actual mistakes,
with targeted drills and an AI tutor that explains every answer choice. It's
what a $100/hour tutor does, for $85/month or $349/year. Your audience is
taking the SAT anyway — you're pointing them to the tool that shows them what
to fix.

## What you'd do

Post 3–5 times a week on your study account — Instagram, TikTok, or both. New
account or existing one; either way it's yours forever. And we do the heavy
lifting:

- **Ready-made carousels** — finished SAT question posts (real questions, real
  traps, clean design). Post as-is or remix.
- **Weekly prompt menus** — 10 hook lines a week to build posts around.
  Prompts, never scripts. Your voice, your edit.
- **The app is your b-roll** — a 15-second screen recording of the "why you
  missed it" screen plus a voiceover is a complete post.
- **Your study life** — study-with-me clips, desk setups, score-goal updates.
  Your account should feel like a person, not an ad. Only about 1 in 3 posts
  mentions SEVA at all.

## How you get paid

You get one personal link — **sevaprep.com/r/yourname** — for your bio and
captions. Anyone who taps it gets **20% off SEVA for 3 months, applied
automatically.** Every subscription from your link is credited to you:

| What happens | You earn |
|---|---|
| Your first post goes live | **$25 starter bonus** |
| Someone signs up annual ($349/yr) through your link | **$70, every time** |
| Someone signs up monthly ($85/mo) through your link | **$15/month for their first 3 months ($45)** |
| You reach 5 total paid signups | **+$50 bonus** |
| You reach 15 total paid signups | **+$150 bonus** |
| You reach 40 total paid signups | **+$400 bonus** |

**How the math works:** your first 5 annual signups = $350 in commissions +
the $50 milestone = **$400.** At 40 annual signups the total is **$3,400.**
There's no cap, and your link never expires — a post from September keeps
paying you in December.

Paid monthly — Zelle, PayPal, or Venmo, no minimum. You also get **full free
access to SEVA** the whole time you're a partner. One honest note: only paid
subscriptions count. Free trials that cancel earn nothing — which is exactly
why your automatic 20% discount matters: it converts the fence-sitters.

## What this is NOT

- **You never pay anything.** No fees, no "starter kits," no buying the
  product.
- **No follower minimum.** We care whether your content is good, not how big
  you are yet.
- **No scripts, no approval process.** Prompts and raw material from us; the
  voice is yours.
- **Not a pyramid.** You never recruit anyone. You earn from subscriptions,
  period.
- **Your content stays yours.** We ask before resharing anything, and credit
  you when we do.
- **Cash, not gift cards.** Ever.

## Beyond the money

Founding partners get a direct line to the founder (a real person who
answers, not a portal), a say in what we build next, "Creator Partner — SEVA"
for your resume and LinkedIn, and a personal reference or recommendation from
the founder when you've been active with us. You're not joining a program;
you're joining the team early.

## The rules (short, and they protect you)

1. **Disclose the partnership.** "#ad" or "Sponsored by SEVA" on promo posts,
   "SEVA partner" in bio. It's the law — and we give you the exact wording
   every time, so you're always covered.
2. **No made-up claims.** Never invent score jumps. We give you true numbers,
   and they're impressive enough.
3. **Your link lives in your bio** and in the caption or pinned comment of
   every promo post.
4. **Stay active to stay in.** 30+ quiet days and we may retire your link so
   a seat opens for someone active.
5. **Under 18?** No problem — a parent or guardian just co-signs a simple
   one-page agreement.
